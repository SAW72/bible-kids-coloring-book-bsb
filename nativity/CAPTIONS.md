# Nativity captions

Exact Berean Standard Bible wording for each scene page. The BSB is public domain.
Text was copied from the Bible Hub BSB pages linked below (the verse block at the top of each chapter, not the commentary).
Footnote letters that Bible Hub inserts inside a verse are not part of the translation and are not printed.

Quotation marks, apostrophes, and capitalization are copied as printed. Where a quotation runs across several verses, a caption that is one whole verse keeps only the marks that belong to that verse.

## Source notes

### 02-gabriel-mary.png

- Scene: Gabriel appears to Mary
- Printed reference: Luke 1:30
- Printed caption: So the angel told her, “Do not be afraid, Mary, for you have found favor with God.
- Source: https://biblehub.com/bsb/luke/1.htm
- Note: The whole verse, as printed on Bible Hub. The angel’s words continue into verse 31, so this verse has an opening quotation mark and no closing mark.

### 03-mary-says-yes.png

- Scene: Mary says yes
- Printed reference: Luke 1:38
- Printed caption: “I am the Lord’s servant,” Mary answered. “May it happen to me according to your word.” Then the angel left her.
- Source: https://biblehub.com/bsb/luke/1.htm
- Note: The whole verse.

### 04-mary-visits-elizabeth.png

- Scene: Mary visits Elizabeth
- Printed reference: Luke 1:42
- Printed caption: In a loud voice she exclaimed, “Blessed are you among women, and blessed is the fruit of your womb!
- Source: https://biblehub.com/bsb/luke/1.htm
- Note: The whole verse. Elizabeth’s words continue into verse 43, so the quotation is still open at the end of this verse.

### 05-joseph-dream.png

- Scene: An angel speaks to Joseph in a dream
- Printed reference: Matthew 1:21
- Printed caption: She will give birth to a Son, and you are to give Him the name Jesus, because He will save His people from their sins.”
- Source: https://biblehub.com/bsb/matthew/1.htm
- Note: The whole verse. Bible Hub prints a footnote letter after “Jesus”; that letter is not part of the BSB text and is omitted. The closing quotation mark belongs to the angel’s speech, which opened in Matthew 1:20.

### 06-caesars-decree.png

- Scene: Caesar’s decree
- Printed reference: Luke 2:1
- Printed caption: Now in those days a decree went out from Caesar Augustus that a census should be taken of the whole empire.
- Source: https://biblehub.com/bsb/luke/2.htm
- Note: The whole verse. Bible Hub prints a footnote letter after the sentence; that letter is not part of the BSB text and is omitted.

### 07-journey-to-bethlehem.png

- Printed reference: Luke 2:5

- Source: https://biblehub.com/bsb/luke/2.htm
- Note: The whole verse.

### 08-no-room-at-the-inn.png

- Scene: No room at the inn
- Printed reference: Luke 2:7
- Printed caption: because there was no room for them in the inn.
- Source: https://biblehub.com/bsb/luke/2.htm
- Note: The closing clause of Luke 2:7, copied with its original lowercase. The birth and manger clauses of the same verse are the caption on the next page.

### 09-baby-in-the-manger.png

- Scene: Jesus born and laid in a manger
- Printed reference: Luke 2:7
- Printed caption: And she gave birth to her firstborn, a Son. She wrapped Him in swaddling cloths and laid Him in a manger
- Source: https://biblehub.com/bsb/luke/2.htm
- Note: The opening of Luke 2:7, through the word “manger,” stopping before the comma that joins the “no room” clause printed on the previous page.
